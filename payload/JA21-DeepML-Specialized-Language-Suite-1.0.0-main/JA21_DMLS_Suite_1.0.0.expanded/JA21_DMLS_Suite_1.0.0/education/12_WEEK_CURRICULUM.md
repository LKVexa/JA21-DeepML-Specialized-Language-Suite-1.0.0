# Twelve-Week DeepML Specialized Curriculum

| Week | Theme | Reading | Applied work |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Language identity, domains, namespaces, and packages | Chapters 1–2 | Trace source-to-evidence chains and publish one local package. |
| 2 | Units, schemas, metadata, provenance, tensors, and adapters | Chapters 3–5 | Build a dimensionally valid data path and paired diagnostic. |
| 3 | Custom operators and kernel identity | Chapter 6 | Author a custom operator profile and hash-mismatch test. |
| 4 | Financial and scientific domains | Chapters 7–8 | Create local risk and scientific workflows with assumptions and uncertainty. |
| 5 | Graphics and robotics domains | Chapters 9–10 | Preserve latent evidence and enforce declaration-only authority. |
| 6 | Simulation, causality, and domain inference | Chapters 11–12 | Build a causal environment and a cross-domain inference graph. |
| 7 | Differentiable simulation and risk–physics coupling | Chapters 13–14 | Publish gradient, stability, uncertainty, and non-collapse evidence. |
| 8 | Calibration and constraints | Chapter 15 | Create valid, unstable, and contradictory calibration cases. |
| 9 | Sparse planning, kernel selection, specialization, and folding | Chapters 16–17 | Prove deterministic optimization and implementation choice. |
| 10 | Target lowering, R12/MCRT, and integrated packages | Chapters 18–19 | Lower and package an integrated local graph. |
| 11 | Safety diagnostics and certification | Chapter 20 | Run a stage-aware diagnostic campaign, then freeze behavior. |
| 12 | 8S profile and standalone capstone | Chapters 21–22 | Publish a bounded implementation and release evidence plan. |

## Assessment

- 20% weekly laboratories
- 20% diagnostic and unit-analysis portfolio
- 20% compiler/runtime evidence notebook
- 40% standalone toolchain capstone and conformance report

A passing capstone must disclose unsupported features and must not present modeled corpus outcomes as native execution evidence.
